# Supplier performance policy

> SIMULATED COMPANY POLICY, written for the ChainPilot demo. It is not the policy of any real company. The suppliers (SYN-SUP codes) are simulated.

Version 1, 2026-10-17.

## Measuring delivery performance

A delivery is on time when a purchase order is received on or before its expected date (the order date plus the supplier's promised lead time). Each supplier's on-time rate is measured over its received orders of the last 90 days:

- 90% or more on time: good.
- From 75% to under 90%: watch.
- Under 75%: at risk.

A supplier with fewer than five received orders in the period is not rated, because a few orders say little.

## What to do about late suppliers

For a supplier rated at risk, planners check its open orders first, especially overdue ones, and consider moving stock between warehouses for the affected products. Changing suppliers is a manager's decision and is outside the system.

## Lead time data

Each supplier has a promised lead time and its standard deviation in days. Both are used in the safety-stock calculation. When a supplier is often late, the lead time data should be updated by an administrator.
