# Stock transfer policy

> SIMULATED COMPANY POLICY, written for the ChainPilot demo. It is not the policy of any real company.

Version 1, 2026-10-17.

## Transfers come first

When a warehouse is out of stock or at risk, stock that another warehouse can spare is moved before anything new is bought from a supplier. Moving spare stock is faster and cheaper than buying, and it reduces excess stock elsewhere. A supplier order is recommended only for what transfers cannot cover.

## Which stock can move

A warehouse may give away only the units it holds above its own target level for that product. A warehouse that is itself short of a product never gives that product away. This protects the sending site from creating a new shortage.

## Space at the receiving site

A transfer must fit into the free space of the receiving warehouse (capacity minus stock on hand minus open orders arriving there). If space is short, the transfer is reduced.

## Approval

A transfer recommendation is a proposal. Stock moves only after a manager approves it. Travel time between warehouses is not planned for yet.
